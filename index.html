TIME CARD QUESTIONS

worth clocking.

Know what goes into your weekly total.

How are overnight hours calculated?

If the end clock time is earlier than the start, we add one day. The Next day checkbox also allows an equal end time to represent 24 hours. Shifts longer than 24 hours are rejected.

How do daily and weekly overtime interact?

Daily overtime is counted first. Weekly overtime is then calculated on the remaining regular hours. This prevents charging the same hour twice, but does not model every region’s payroll rules.

Are unpaid breaks deducted?

Yes. Enter total unpaid break minutes for each shift. A break cannot exceed the shift duration. Paid breaks should not be entered as unpaid time.

Does this handle daylight saving time?

No. Inputs are clock times without dates or time zones. A shift crossing a daylight-saving transition may have a different actual elapsed duration.
