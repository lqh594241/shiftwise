Local processing

Shiftwise processes files and inputs in your browser. The tool does not upload your content, require an account or save your inputs to local storage. Analytics is described below. Analytics may process page URLs, referrers, browser or device information and connection data. The tool does not intentionally send your input text or file contents to Ahrefs. See Ahrefs privacy information for its data practices. Advertising locations remain empty; no advertising scripts are enabled.

Hosting

Your hosting provider may process IP addresses, requested URLs and ordinary device or connection information. External links, if used, are governed by the destination site’s policies.

Your choices

Use only files you are comfortable opening on this device. You control which results you copy, print or download. No file content is sent to a remote processing service by this version.
